# GitLab Overview

GitLab is where students write code, submit assignments, and collaborate on projects. The integration makes sure everyone lands in the right place with the right permissions — without manual setup.

## What you see when you log in

When a teacher or student clicks **Sign in with Authentik**, GitLab checks who they are and sends them straight to their dashboard. There is no separate password to remember.

Once inside, they see the year groups they belong to. Each year group works like an organization on GitHub: it contains projects, members, and settings that are shared across everything inside it.

## What teachers see

A teacher in **sk25-26** sees:

- The **sk25-26** group in their sidebar
- Every project created by any student in that group
- The ability to open, review, and contribute to any of those projects
- Merge request buttons and admin settings for the group

They do not see projects from **sk26-27** unless those projects are explicitly shared.

## What students see

A student in **sk25-26** sees:

- The **sk25-26** group in their sidebar
- Their own projects and any projects classmates have invited them to
- The ability to create new projects inside the group
- An **Invite members** button on their projects to add other students as collaborators

Students cannot create projects outside the year group. Everything they make lives inside **sk25-26** where teachers can see it.

## How projects work

| Action | Who can do it |
|--------|---------------|
| Create a new project inside the year group | Students and teachers |
| Make a project private so teachers cannot see it | No one — all projects are at least Internal |
| Invite another student to collaborate | Students (up to Developer level) |
| Change group settings or merge any MR | Teachers only |
| Delete someone else's project | Teachers only |

## What happens behind the scenes

A background sync service checks Authentik every few minutes and keeps GitLab up to date:

- New year groups appear automatically
- New members are added with the correct role
- People who left a year group are removed
- Any project that somehow became private is flipped back to visible

You do not interact with the sync service directly. It simply ensures that what you set up in Authentik is reflected in GitLab.

## Common questions

**Can I work with someone from another year group?**
Yes, but only if they are explicitly invited to your specific project. Year groups are separate by default.

**What if my teacher changes classes?**
Your admin moves them to the new year group in Authentik. On the next sync, they lose access to your old group and gain access to the new one.

**Can I leave the year group?**
You can click **Leave group**, but the sync service will add you back within a few minutes. If you truly need to be removed, ask your admin to remove you from the year group in Authentik.

**Do I need a GitLab password?**
No. You log in through Authentik every time. Your GitLab account is created automatically if it does not exist yet.
